Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

StagePilot is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner, on their own computer. It has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's sign-in, StagePilot reads the owner's Gmail messages (scope gmail.readonly) and may send e-mails from the owner's mailbox (scope gmail.send).

How the data is used

  • Only e-mails related to the owner's internship search (recruiters, firms, recruiting platforms) are kept; all other e-mails are ignored and not stored.
  • An e-mail is sent only when the owner clicks « Send » or schedules it. Nothing is sent automatically on anyone else's behalf.
  • Nothing in the mailbox is deleted, moved, labelled or marked as read.

Storage and sharing

  • Kept data is stored only in a local database on the owner's computer. The Google access token is stored encrypted there.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and none is used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • StagePilot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's « Third-party apps & services » page; StagePilot then stops reading the mailbox.

Contact

Questions: the support e-mail address shown on StagePilot's Google consent screen.
