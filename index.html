StagePilot

StagePilot is a personal, single-user tool that helps its owner organise an internship search: offers, applications, contacts and the related e-mails.

It runs only on its owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

With the owner's consent, it reads the owner's own Gmail mailbox to file the e-mails related to the internship search, and sends an e-mail only when the owner clicks « Send » or schedules it.
